DocFlare AI

Open-source, zero-cost AI site search & chatbot widget. 100% Cloudflare native.

This Worker is running. The chat bubble in the corner is the embeddable widget served from /widget.js. Index a site, then point the widget at it with data-site-id.

1. Index your documentation

curl -X POST "$WORKER_URL/api/sites/index" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"siteId":"demo","sitemapUrl":"https://docs.example.com/sitemap.xml"}'

2. Embed the widget

<script src="$WORKER_URL/widget.js" data-site-id="demo" data-theme="auto" defer></script>

3. Ask questions

Open the bubble in the bottom-right corner and ask something. Answers stream in with their sources.
